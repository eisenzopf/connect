Gate 5 · G5-046 · D5-05

Independent first-run walkthroughs

Protocol and blank record only. No participant attempt or usability pass is recorded here.

The approved release profile requires at least three independent fresh users. Each follows the delivered instructions on the exact candidate, exercises the agent and app journeys, and records the first local message and reply. Installation and host/model authentication are measured separately.

Prepare the candidate

  1. Wait for the candidate's matching package qualification to pass. Record its source revision, archive SHA-256, installed manifest SHA-256, and qualification report SHA-256. Use the same candidate across attempts; record any later replacement explicitly.
  2. Choose three people who did not author or rehearse this implementation. Use anonymous labels P01, P02 and P03. Record relevant experience and prior exposure without publishing names or contact details.
  3. Give each person a fresh isolated local installation and synthetic conversation content. Supply the delivered first-ten-minutes guide, app guide, and selected-host guide. Avoid undocumented setup or private developer tools.
  4. Record host/model authentication and any other prerequisites before judging the local journey. Model usage requires the participant's chosen authenticated host. Use local fixtures for provider prerequisites; this walkthrough does not authorize calls, SMS, public ingress, or provider account changes.

Participant brief

Using the delivered instructions, install Connect and establish the documented local conversation. Send a message and receive its reply. Exercise both the selected agent host and the reference app. Tell the observer when instructions are unclear, a step fails, or you need help. Keep every failed attempt; restarting is part of the record.

After the first reply, inspect the same conversation through the other journey, end and reopen the documented session, and check that the prior conversation remains available. Record the reference-client experience and any missing or confusing status.

Record the clocks and observations

PhaseWhat to record
InstallationStart, finish, elapsed seconds, platform, failures, retries, and any help. Include download/build time when applicable; identify the installation method.
PrerequisitesSelected host/model readiness and authentication, media tools or permissions, and local provider-fixture readiness. Record each interval and any overlap with installation.
Local message/replyRecord installation-complete time, all-prerequisites-ready time, journey start, first send, and first observed reply. Report both wall time since installation and the ready-to-start journey duration. Retain pauses and their reasons rather than subtracting them silently.
Agent and appRecord both journey outcomes, conversation continuity, instructions consulted, reference-client issues, restarts, and facilitator interventions.
CleanupStop only attempt-owned processes and remove only test-owned credentials/state. Keep private evidence and failure records under ignored .local/.

The target is a local message and reply within 600 seconds after installation, with prerequisite timing stated separately. A participant needing coaching is an assisted attempt; do not describe it as an unassisted first run. A missed target remains a miss unless the claim is explicitly reviewed. Fix blocking instructions and repeat affected walkthroughs with a separately identified attempt.

Evidence and completion

Copy the blank record into an ignored run directory and add one record per attempt. Record timestamps with timezone offsets and durations in seconds. Preserve original records before any correction.

Publish a redacted summary that binds all attempts to their exact candidate and includes failures, restarts, assistance and actual timings. Credentials, personal identifiers, raw logs and model transcripts remain private. Screenshots or journal references may support observed delivery; an observer script is not an independent participant.

G5-046 is complete only after the required independent walkthroughs and remediation evidence exist. This kit supplies the procedure, not the human result. Owner acceptance of the exact candidate and publication remain separate actions.
