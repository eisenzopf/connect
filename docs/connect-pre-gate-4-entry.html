Preserve the product boundaries

The protocol defines optional safe health data; the service exposes authorized observations; the server owns workers and registration supervision; the store owns durable policy and receipts. Connector descriptors live in the connector API. Provider callbacks and live state are not changed by this qualification.

The conversation survives calls and transport changes. A task will bind delegated work to that conversation. Two tasks can share a conversation without consuming each other’s replies; authority must come from the server, never a model-provided identifier.
