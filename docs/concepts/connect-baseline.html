04 / THE VALUE

CPaaS connects you to services.

Providers supply network access, numbers, messaging, voice, and managed capabilities.

Connect helps you bring them together.

An open runtime combines supported services inside a conversation your application controls.

Use a carrier for telephone access, Vapi for voice AI, and a browser for human participation—all through the same communications model.

Fewer pieces to operate.

Built-in communications machinery reduces the gateway stack you assemble.

Choose where it runs.

Embed the library, operate Connect, or use a compatible hosted service.

Choose the services behind it.

Compose providers through shared operations and declared capabilities.

Keep the conversation together.

Change channels or devices without rebuilding its identity in every integration.

CPaaS platforms also offer orchestration. Connect’s proposed distinction is the combination of an embeddable engine, independent operation, an open interface, and community connectors.
