What the authorization cleanup did—and did not prove

Before v215, 121 focused checks and both shipping builds passed. In the complete run, replacement/retirement failures decreased from 27 to 25 and three reclamation checks began passing. But workload failures increased from 5 to 11. Across all 432 checks, 11 gained a pass and 12 lost a pass; the total changed from 288 to 287. These are observations from different runs, not a controlled experiment proving which change caused them. There is no measured before/after database-query reduction in this evidence.

The verification gap was material: the representative signed test exercised AV for 26 seconds, but omitted the matrix’s post-commit Data sequence and full retirement duration. Passing that test was insufficient evidence of readiness. The new plan requires those concurrent operations and original deadlines in the regression workload.

The boundary we will enforce

Control planePrepare transport and codecs → validate current authority → activate once. Journal results and status without invalidating unchanged media permissions.
Media planeAdmitted stream → local current-generation/expiry check → encode, forward and render. Unrelated control transactions must not interrupt healthy admitted media.

Only a relevant authority or ownership change crosses the boundary. Revocation, expiry, membership loss, ownership transfer and stream-policy changes invalidate the affected grant. Unrelated participant bookkeeping does not refresh every participant. Packet custody whose authorization was genuinely revoked must never be revived.

  • Prepare transport and codecs independently; authorize and activate at one controlled boundary. Keep the existing media connection active during preparation.
  • Admitted packet processing uses a current immutable grant plus bounded local generation/expiry checks. Unrelated control bookkeeping must not invalidate media grants or require SQL on each packet.
  • Only relevant authority, ownership, membership, stream-policy and expiry changes invalidate affected grants. Revoked grants and old queued custody are never revived.
  • Busy preparation waits within its original deadline and retains its original owner. It never repeats a physical mutation or extends the acceptance deadline.
  • Session admission alone is insufficient for perpetual authority: changes remain enforceable, while healthy unchanged media avoids redundant control-plane work.
